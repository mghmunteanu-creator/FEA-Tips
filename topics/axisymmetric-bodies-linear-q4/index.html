The four-node isoparametric finite element for axisymmetric bodies is very similar to the element used for a two-dimensional plane state. The development therefore follows Section 9.4.

The strains, stresses and displacements are defined below. The yyy-axis is the axis of symmetry, and xxx denotes the radial coordinate rrr.

In natural coordinates:

kel=∫−11∫−11x(s,t)B(s,t)TDB(s,t)det⁡ ⁣(J(s,t)) ds dt\boldsymbol{k}_{el}=\int_{-1}^{1}\int_{-1}^{1}x(s,t)\boldsymbol{B}(s,t)^{T}\boldsymbol{D}\boldsymbol{B}(s,t)\det\!\left(\boldsymbol{J}(s,t)\right)\,ds\,dtkel​=∫−11​∫−11​x(s,t)B(s,t)TDB(s,t)det(J(s,t))dsdt

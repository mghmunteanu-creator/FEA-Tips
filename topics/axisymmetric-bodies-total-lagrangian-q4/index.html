In the Total Lagrangian formulation, the initial, undeformed geometry is the reference configuration. All derivatives, Jacobians and integrals are evaluated with respect to that configuration; see Section 12.2. The four-node interpolation and axisymmetric coordinate convention follow Section 28.1.

The Green–Lagrange strain vector is denoted by ε\boldsymbol{\varepsilon}ε. The stress vector σ\boldsymbol{\sigma}σ contains the energetically conjugate second Piola–Kirchhoff stresses. The yyy-axis is the axis of symmetry and xxx is the radial coordinate.

Important remark. The Total Lagrangian formulation uses the initial configuration as reference and does not require incremental updating of that reference configuration. This does not make the numerical solution exact: the nonlinear equilibrium equations are solved iteratively. Load increments may be needed to improve convergence and follow the equilibrium path. Provided that the same converged equilibrium solution is reached, the total strain is evaluated directly from the initial configuration, rather than by accumulating strain increments. Accuracy and convergence must therefore be distinguished.

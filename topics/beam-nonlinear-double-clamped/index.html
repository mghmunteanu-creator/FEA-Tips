01

Model and constitutive relations

A double-clamped beam is loaded at its midpoint by the force FFF. The material is linear elastic, strains are small, and displacements are moderately large [2].

Points AAA and BBB are completely fixed. Because the axial displacements of both ends are restrained, an axial force develops as the beam deflects.

MATLAB and ANSYS files

The MATLAB package contains the complete nonlinear analysis, result recovery, diagram generation, and a README. The code follows the element equations above: deriv.m evaluates ε0\varepsilon_0ε0​, κ\kappaκ, and their derivatives; stiff.m integrates and assembles fel\boldsymbol f_{el}fel​ and kel\boldsymbol k_{el}kel​; and main.m performs the load-step and Newton–Raphson loops.

deriv.mStrain and curvature
%*** deriv ***
ep = Nux*uel + 1/2*(Nvx*uel)^2;
ep1 = Nux' + (Nvx'*Nvx)*uel;
ep2 = Nvx'*Nvx;

ka = Nka*uel;
ka1 = Nka';

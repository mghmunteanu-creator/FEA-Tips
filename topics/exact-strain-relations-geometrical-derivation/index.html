Geometrical Derivation of the Exact Strain Relations

A direct geometrical derivation of the exact relations between the Green–Lagrange strain components and the changes in length and angle of two initially orthogonal material fibers.

Consider the two initially orthogonal material fibers shown in Figure 1. The exact geometrical strains are the relative changes in their lengths, exe_xex​ and eye_yey​, and the decrease αxy\alpha_{xy}αxy​ of the initial right angle between them.

01

Axial strain relations

The Green–Lagrange normal strain components are:

Exx=u,x+12u,x,2+12v,x,2E_{xx}=u_{,x}+\frac12u_{,x}^{,2}+\frac12v_{,x}^{,2}Exx​=u,x​+21​u,x,2​+21​v,x,2​
Eyy=v,y+12u,y,2+12v,y,2E_{yy}=v_{,y}+\frac12u_{,y}^{,2}+\frac12v_{,y}^{,2}Eyy​=v,y​+21​u,y,2​+21​v,y,2​

The exact relative changes in length obtained in Chapter 11 are:

ex=(1+u,x)2+v,x,2−1e_x=\sqrt{(1+u_{,x})^2+v_{,x}^{,2}}-1ex​=(1+u,x​)2+v,x,2​​−1
ey=u,y,2+(1+v,y)2−1e_y=\sqrt{u_{,y}^{,2}+(1+v_{,y})^2}-1ey​=u,y,2​+(1+v,y​)2​−1

Therefore:

Exx=ex+12ex2E_{xx}=e_x+\frac12e_x^2Exx​=ex​+21​ex2​

Shear strain relation

The scalar product of the two deformed material fibers is:

1′2′‾ ⁣⋅ ⁣1′3′‾=[u,y+v,x+u,xu,y+v,xv,y]dx dy\begin{aligned}\overline{1'2'}\!\cdot\!\overline{1'3'}={}&\bigl[u_{,y}+v_{,x}\\[-1pt]&+u_{,x}u_{,y}+v_{,x}v_{,y}\bigr]dx\,dy\end{aligned}1′2′⋅1′3′=​[u,y​+v,x​+u,x​u,y​+v,x​v,y​]dxdy​

2Exy=u,y+v,x+u,xu,y+v,xv,y2E_{xy}=u_{,y}+v_{,x}+u_{,x}u_{,y}+v_{,x}v_{,y}2Exy​=u,y​+v,x​+u,x​u,y​+v,x​v,y​

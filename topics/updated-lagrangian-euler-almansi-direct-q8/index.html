01

Q8 extension

This section extends the direct evaluation of Euler–Almansi strains presented in Section 15.3 to the Q8 element. The element has eight nodes, 16 element displacement DOFs and quadratic interpolation. It uses 3×33\times33×3 Gauss quadrature.

The remaining Updated Lagrangian formulation, including B0\boldsymbol B_0B0​, BL\boldsymbol B_LBL​, the geometrical stiffness terms and the Newton–Raphson procedure, is unchanged from the formulation already presented in Section 15.3.

02

Direct Euler–Almansi strain

With J0\boldsymbol J_0J0​ and Jt\boldsymbol J_tJt​ evaluated from the initial and current trial nodal coordinates, respectively, the total deformation gradient is:

Ftot=(J0−1Jt)T.\boldsymbol F_{\mathrm{tot}}=\left(\boldsymbol J_0^{-1}\boldsymbol J_t\right)^T.Ftot​=(J0−1​Jt​)T.

The left Cauchy–Green tensor and the Euler–Almansi strain tensor are:

b=FtotFtotT,\boldsymbol b=\boldsymbol F_{\mathrm{tot}}\boldsymbol F_{\mathrm{tot}}^T,b=Ftot​FtotT​,
e=12(I−b−1).\boldsymbol e=\frac12\left(\boldsymbol I-\boldsymbol b^{-1}\right).e=21​(I−b−1).

The engineering components are:

ex=e11,ey=e22,γxy=2e12.e_x=e_{11},\qquad e_y=e_{22},\qquad\gamma_{xy}=2e_{12}.ex​=e11​,ey​=e22​,γxy​=2e12​.

The Q8 element uses nine Gauss points, formed from the following coordinates in each natural-coordinate direction:

s,t=−3/5,  0,  +3/5,s,t=-\sqrt{3/5},\;0,\;+\sqrt{3/5},s,t=−3/5​,0,+3/5​,

with the corresponding one-dimensional weights:

5/9,  8/9,  5/9.5/9,\;8/9,\;5/9.5/9,8/9,5/9.

The Q8 element converges much faster than Q4 for this example. The coarse Q8 mesh 20×320\times320×3, containing only 60 elements, already gives results very close to the finest Q8 result. In contrast, the Q4 mesh 180×24180\times24180×24 contains 4320 elements and still gives slightly smaller displacement values.
